IELTS SPEAKING PART 1 - BÀI MẪ U CHO 20 CHỦ ĐỀ
1. Hometown

Where is your hometown?
Keywords: quiet, peaceful, friendly people
 My hometown is a small city in Vietnam. It’s a quiet and peaceful place with a lot of trees and beautiful
landscapes. People here are friendly and always ready to help each other.

What do you like about your hometown?
Keywords: fresh air, not crowded/noisy, delicious food
I like the fresh air and the relaxing atmosphere. Unlike big cities, my hometown is not too crowded or
noisy. Also, the food here is delicious and quite affordable.

Is your hometown a good place to live?
Keywords: fresh air, not crowded/noisy, fewer jobs
Yes, I think it’s a great place to live. It’s safe, has a lower cost of living compared to major cities, and
has a strong sense of community. However, job opportunities can be limited.

2. Study

Do you work or study?
Keywords: English, prepare for IELTS
I am a student. I am currently studying English because I want to improve my language skills and
prepare for the IELTS exam.

Why did you choose this major?
Keywords: English, useful for future: people + jobs
I chose to study English because it is an international language and will be useful for my future. It can
help me communicate with people from different countries and open up more job opportunities.

Do you like your studies?
Keywords: yes, challenges + interesting
Yes, I really enjoy studying English. It is challenging but also very interesting. However, sometimes it
can be stressful when there are too many lessons to learn.

3. Daily Routine

What do you usually do in a day?
Keywords: wake up -> breakfast -> work; evening: books + TV; weekends: family + friends
On weekdays, I wake up early, have breakfast, and go to work. In the evening, I like to relax by reading
books or watching TV. On weekends, I spend time with my family and friends.

Do you have a fixed daily routine?
Keywords: schedule -> organized; small changes
Yes, I follow a regular schedule because it helps me stay organized. However, sometimes I make small
changes, like going out for dinner or exercising in the morning instead of the evening.

What part of the day do you enjoy the most?
Keywords: evening -> relax (movies, books)
I enjoy the evening the most because I can relax after a long day. It’s the time when I can watch a
movie, read a book, or just spend time with my loved ones.

4. Hobbies

What hobbies do you have?
Keywords: books + music + traveling
I enjoy reading books, listening to music, and traveling. These activities help me relax and learn new
things at the same time.

How often do you do your hobbies?
Keywords: books: every day; music: free time; travelling: a few times/year
I read books almost every day, and I listen to music whenever I have free time. As for traveling, I try to
do it a few times a year when I have a break from work.

Do you think hobbies are important?
Keywords: yes -> relax + ß stress
Yes, hobbies are very important because they help people relax and reduce stress. They also allow
people to develop new skills and meet like-minded individuals.

5. Food

What kind of food do you like?
Keywords: Vietnamese food -> fresh + healthy
I love Vietnamese food because it’s fresh, healthy, and full of flavor. Some of my favorites are pho,
banh mi, and fresh spring rolls.

Do you prefer eating at home or in restaurants?
Keywords: at home -> healthier + affordable
 I prefer eating at home because home-cooked food is healthier and more affordable. But sometimes, I
like eating out to try new dishes.

What is a popular dish in your country?
Keywords: Pho: beef/chicken + broth
Pho is one of the most famous dishes in Vietnam. It’s a noodle soup with beef or chicken, and it has a
delicious, flavorful broth.

6. Weather

What’s the weather like in your country?
Keywords: tropical; North: 4 seasons, South: hot
Vietnam has a tropical climate. The north has four seasons, while the south is hot all year round.
Summers are very hot, and winters can be quite cold in some areas.

What’s your favorite season?
Keywords: autumn: cool + comfortable
I like autumn because the weather is cool and comfortable. It’s neither too hot nor too cold, and the
air feels fresh.

Do you think climate change is affecting the weather?
Keywords: yes; storms + floods
Yes, I think so. The summers are getting hotter, and there are more unexpected storms and floods. It’s
becoming more extreme than before.

7. Shopping

Do you like shopping?
Keywords: yes, careful with $
Yes, I enjoy shopping, especially for clothes and books. It’s fun to explore different styles and find
good deals. However, I try to be careful with my spending and only buy what I really need.

Do you prefer shopping online or in stores?
Keywords: online; convenient; shoes + clothes: stores
I prefer online shopping because it’s more convenient. I can compare prices and read reviews before
buying. However, for clothes and shoes, I like going to stores to try them on first.

What’s the last thing you bought?
Keywords: book, personal development
The last thing I bought was a new book about personal development. I love reading in my free time
because it helps me learn new things.

8. Technology

Do you like using technology?
Keywords: yes; work + entertainment
Yes, I use technology every day for work, study, and entertainment. It makes life easier and helps me
stay connected with people.

What is your favorite gadget?
Keywords: smartphone - everything
My smartphone is my favorite gadget because I can use it for everything—communication, learning,
and entertainment. It’s very useful in daily life.

Is technology changing the way we live?
Keywords: yes – everything online + affect health
 Yes, it has changed everything. Now, we can work, study, and shop online. However, some people
spend too much time on their phones, which is not good for their health.

9. Travel

Do you like traveling?
Keywords: yes, new places/people/cultures
Yes, I love traveling because it allows me to explore new places, meet new people, and experience
different cultures. It’s always exciting to see something new.

Where would you like to travel in the future?
Keywords: Japan, scenery, history, food
 I’d love to visit Japan because of its beautiful scenery, rich history, and delicious food. The culture
there is very interesting.

Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?
Keywords: family & friends - safer
 I prefer traveling with friends or family because it’s more fun and feels safer. However, sometimes
traveling alone can be a good way to enjoy personal time.

10. Music

What kind of music do you like?
Keywords: pop + acoustic
I enjoy pop and acoustic music. Pop songs are catchy and fun, while acoustic music helps me relax.

When do you usually listen to music?
Keywords: free + studying + travelling
I listen to music in my free time, especially when I’m studying or traveling. It helps me focus and
makes me feel happy.

Can music change your mood?
Keywords: yes – upbeat: energetic, slow: relax
Yes, definitely! Upbeat music makes me feel energetic, while slow songs help me relax after a long day.

11. Accommodation

Do you live in a house or an apartment?
Keywords: apartment – small, comfortable
 I live in an apartment. It’s small but comfortable, and it has everything I need. The best thing is that it’s
located near my workplace, so it’s very convenient.

What do you like about your home?
Keywords: cozy, safe neighborhood, nice view
I like that it’s cozy and quiet. The neighborhood is safe, and my apartment has a nice view from the
balcony. It’s a great place to relax after a long day.

Would you like to move to a different home in the future?
Keywords: yes, bigger + garden + outdoor area
Yes, in the future, I’d love to live in a bigger house with a garden. I think having more space would be
nice, and I’d love to have a small outdoor area to enjoy fresh air.

12. Friends

Do you have many friends?
Keywords: small group, quality > quantity
I have a small group of close friends. I prefer quality over quantity because I believe real friendship is
about trust and understanding.

How often do you meet your friends?
Keywords: 1/week – coffee, dinner, talk
I meet my friends at least once a week. We usually go out for coffee, have dinner together, or just hang
out and talk.

What do you usually do with your friends?
Keywords: movies, trips
 We like to chat, watch movies, and sometimes go on short trips. Spending time with friends helps me
relax and feel happy.

13. Pets

Do you have a pet?
Keywords: no, future: dog/cat
No, I don’t have a pet at the moment, but I would love to have one in the future, maybe a dog or a cat.

What pet would you like to have?
Keywords: dog - loyal
I would like to have a dog because they are loyal and playful. I think having a dog would be great for
companionship and exercise.

Do you think pets are important?
Keywords: happiness + reduce stress
Yes, I think pets are important because they bring happiness and help reduce stress. They also teach
people responsibility and care.

14. Movies

Do you like watching movies?
Keywords: yes - relax
Yes, I love watching movies, especially in my free time. It’s a great way to relax and enjoy a good story.

What kind of movies do you like?
Keywords: action + comedy - laugh
I enjoy action and comedy movies. Action movies are exciting and full of energy, while comedies make
me laugh and feel happy.

Do you prefer watching movies at home or in the cinema?
Keywords: home – comfortable; cinema: new releases
I prefer watching movies at home because it’s more comfortable and I can pause whenever I want. But
sometimes, I like going to the cinema to watch new releases.

15. Sports

Do you like playing sports?
Keywords: yes -> fit + healthy
 Yes, I like playing sports because it helps me stay fit and healthy. It’s also a great way to have fun with
friends.

What is your favorite sport?
Keywords: badminton -> easy
 I enjoy playing badminton because it’s easy to play and a great way to exercise. I also like watching
football on TV.

Do you prefer playing or watching sports?
Keywords: play -> active; watch: tournaments
I prefer playing because it keeps me active. But I also enjoy watching important matches, especially
during big tournaments.

16. Reading

Do you like reading books?

Yes, I love reading because it helps me learn new things and improves my imagination. I usually read

What kind of books do you like to read?
Keywords: novels + self-help
I like reading novels and self-help books. Novels are entertaining, and self-help books help me grow.

17. Transportation

What kind of transport do you usually use?
Keywords: motorbikes -> fast + convenient
I usually use a motorbike because it’s fast and convenient in the city. It helps me avoid traffic jams.

What is the most popular means of transport in your country?
Keywords: motorbikes -> affordable + easy
Motorbikes are the most common because they are affordable and easy to use in busy streets. Many

Do you like public transportation?

Not really, because it’s sometimes crowded and slow. But I think it’s a good option for saving money

19. Weekend Activities

What do you usually do on weekends?
Keywords: friends + coffee – home + movies/books
I usually relax, meet friends, or go out for coffee. Sometimes, I just stay home and watch movies or
read books.

Do you prefer spending weekends indoors or outdoors?
Keywords: mood: explore new places - rest
It depends on my mood. Sometimes I like going out to explore new places, but other times I prefer

Do you think weekends are important?
Keywords: relax +recharge
Yes, weekends help people relax and recharge after a busy week. It’s a good time to spend with family

Do you think people use their phones too much?

Yes, many people spend too much time on their phones, which is not good for their health. It’s
